# Kingdom of God (Victoria 3 Mod)

### IMPORTANT: This mod is dependent on the Community Mod Framework to work correctly!
**Community Mod Framework**: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3385002128

*"We believe in one God, the Father Almighty, Maker of all things visible and invisible..." — Nicene Creed, 325AD*

**Kingdom of God** is a flavor mod that aims to implement mechanics for simulating the role of the Christian faiths during the Victorian Age. This mod is planned to have a broad scope eventually encompassing the Roman Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, Lutheran, and Anglican churches.

## Current Content Scope
### Scripted Content Scope:
The following faiths are in-scope:
- Roman Catholicism
- Eastern Orthodoxy

The following faiths will eventually come into scope:
- Oriental Orthodoxy
- European Lutheranism (this will include content for Calvinism)
- Anglicanism

### Mechanical Scope:
The current mechanics are in-scope:
- Local metropolitan / national church content
- Subdivision of faiths via liturgical rites and languages on a per-country basis
- Canon laws managed on a per-faith basis
- Schism mechanics managed on a per-country basis
- Encyclicals as contextless journal entries on a per-faith basis
- Ecumenical councils as contextless journal entries on a per-faith basis
- College of Cardinals (or equivalent body) on a per-faith basis include Papal elections as appropriate
- Episcopal Seats as minor political entities separate from the overall church body

The following mechanics will eventually come into scope:
- The role of priests in colonization (particularly relevant for Russia)
- Mechanics for the preservation and veneration of Holy Sites by the state

### Notes:
- **This mod will not provide any DLC-exclusive content unless you own the respective DLC!**
- Protestantism at large (non-Lutheran and non-Anglican) is *OUT OF SCOPE*

## Compatibility
**Kingdom of God is broadly compatible with other mods and does not overwrite any base game files**
- KoG does not conflict with Better Politics Mod, but it does not support its custom ideologies and is not balanced against its mechanics
- KoG adds support for the Elysian Orthodox faith in Basileia Romaion
